COMPANY PROFILE
MEDIA TULISAN MANUSIA

Integrated Literary, Publishing, and Legality Ecosystem

1 About Us

Media Tulisan Manusia is an integrated literary ecosystem and publishing solution headquartered in East Java. Born out of a concern for the lack of publication space and legal protection for creators, we are here to offer more than just publishing services. As an exclusive platform, we are dedicated to elevating literature to a higher level, ensuring every work receives the best appreciation, while serving as a professional growth space for writers, academics, and startup business owners.

Headquartered in Malang City under the auspices of Tulisanmanusia Group, with operational bases in Sukorejo, Pasuruan, we unite various strategic services under one roof. We combine literary work publication, physical and digital book publishing, Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) protection, to professional business legality management. To date, more than 100 clients from diverse backgrounds have entrusted the guidance and legalisation of their works to our ecosystem.

Our main commitment relies on digital efficiency, workflow transparency, and strict curation quality. All manuscripts and permits are carefully managed by a trusted team, allowing creators to fully focus on the creative process. We ensure that all aspects of legality and distribution are completed with the highest standards.

Therefore, we invite you writers, academics, and innovators to step forward with us. Do not let your grand ideas remain just drafts; let's turn those concepts into real, high-value works that are fully legally protected. Together with Media Tulisan Manusia, let's build a professional and widely impactful literary track record.

2 Vision & Mission

Vision

To become the leading literacy ecosystem and legality companion in Indonesia dedicated to protecting, empowering, and ensuring that every idea possesses professional value and legal certainty.

Mission
  • Publication: Provide a curated digital platform for novice and professional writers to validate and widely distribute their works.
  • National Publishing: Publish high-end literature equipped with officially recognized Quick Response Code Book Number (QRCBN) identification standards.
  • IPR Protection: Educate the public and process legal protection for creators' Copyright Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) at the Directorate General of Intellectual Property (DJKI) of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights to prevent plagiarism practices.
  • Business Legality: Facilitate basic legality for business actors, creators, and MSMEs through mentoring on the issuance of Business Identification Numbers (NIB) integrated with the OSS system.

4 Our Services

We operate through four service pillars specifically designed to support your work from the conceptual stage to legal recognition:

Digital Literary Media Platform

A curated digital publication space accommodating short-form literary works (short stories, pentigraphs, poetry, articles, and news). This platform serves as a crucible for writers to reach wider readers.

QRCBN-Standardized Book Publishing

Comprehensive print and digital manuscript publishing services. Each book is carefully processed through editing stages, layout, exclusive cover design, and equipped with QRCBN for official indexing.

Consultation & IPR Protection

Intellectual Property Rights registration assistance services. We ensure every original idea and copyrighted work of yours obtains valid state legal coverage to prevent unilateral claims.

OSS NIB Creation Services

Online Single Submission (OSS) system mentoring services for creators, writers, and MSME actors wanting to secure their business legality and operational permits quickly and precisely.
